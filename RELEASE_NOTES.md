**Open your webcam. Find out how shrimp you are.**

v0.1 is the first public release. A local-first, open-source webcam app that detects when your desk job turns you into a shrimp. Built with MediaPipe Pose Landmarker + React + Vite. Runs entirely in the browser.

## Features

- **Shrimp Scan** — Real-time posture detection: forward head, stillness, shoulder imbalance, screen distance, lighting
- **Shrimp Score** — 0-100 weighted score, 6 levels (Human → Final Shrimp)
- **Roast Engine** — 200+ roasts across 6 tones, 6 levels, 2 languages
- **Unshrimp Protocol** — 30s Neck Reset, Stand Up 60s, Look Away 20s
- **Share Card** — PNG export with stats + privacy guarantee
- **Daily Damage Report** — Peak score, worst window, top causes, tomorrow's strategy
- **Ask My Body** — Natural language query engine for posture patterns
- **IndexedDB** — All sessions stored locally on device
- **PWA** — Installable to desktop, notification support
- **Phone Companion** — QR pairing + BroadcastChannel sync
- **Agent Policy** — Context-aware interruptions (meeting-safe, night mode, cooldown)
- **i18n** — English + 简体中文

## Privacy

No video upload. No face storage. No emotion detection. No productivity scoring. No boss dashboard. 100% local.
